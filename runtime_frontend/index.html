Incident policy & saved scenes

A demo incident means a density or queue trigger exceeded for 3 consecutive seconds. These are test thresholds, not crowd-safety standards.

Density is initial people / summed route-corridor area. Overlapping streams are counted separately. Source delays: camera 2s, sensor 1s, warden 5s. Slower processing slows the clock; no ticks are skipped.
